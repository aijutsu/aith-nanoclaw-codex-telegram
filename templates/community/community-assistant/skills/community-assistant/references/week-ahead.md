# Week Ahead

Look across the whole coming week so nothing sneaks up: events that still need organising, things
to book now rather than on the day, volunteer gaps, loans falling due, and requests that have been
open long enough to need a decision. This is the wider net the daily brief deliberately leaves out
— the brief handles today, the week-ahead is the planning view. Fires weekly at the day and time
the community set (a recurring task), and runs on ask any time.

## Gather

Query Notion for the next seven days and a bit beyond:

- **The week's events**: Events where `When` falls in the coming week, whatever their `Status` —
  an `Idea` four days out is exactly the thing this run exists to catch.
- **What needs doing before it's too late**: anything with `Helpers needed` still filled, anything
  `Planned` but not `Announced`, anything needing a venue booked or a thing borrowed. Give it
  enough lead time to actually act; flagging a booking the day before is useless.
- **Loans due this week**: Loans `Out` with `Due back` inside the window, so a return is a heads-up
  rather than a chase.
- **Requests worth a decision**: `Open` requests old enough that the honest move is to either find
  an owner or mark them `Dropped`. Say which you think it is.
- **What memory knows is coming**: the annual thing, the seasonal clear-up, the neighbour who
  always needs a hand in the first week of the month. Notion won't have it if nobody logged it —
  that's what your memory is for. Offer to log it as an event.

## Compose

- **Organise, don't just list.** For each event, the question is "what would stop this happening?"
  — no venue, no helpers, nobody's announced it, the one person who owns it is away. Name the
  blocker and propose the fix.
- **Propose owners, never assign them.** Where something needs a person, suggest one from Members'
  `Can offer` and put it as a question to the room or to them. A name in a database is not a
  commitment, and volunteering someone publicly is a good way to lose a volunteer.
- **Draft what wants drafting.** If an event is `Planned` but unannounced, offer the announcement
  — show the draft, don't post it (ground rule 2).
- **Build the "needs doing" list** from everything gathered, and keep it to what can actually be
  done this week.

## Output

```
🗓️ Week ahead: <date range>

Needs doing this week  (lead with the actionable)
- <action>: by <when> <(owner, if there is one)> · <the blocker, plainly>
- ...

The week
Mon: <event> at <place> · <organiser> · <n> going
Tue: ...
- ...

Coming back
- <item> from <borrower>: due <day>

Still open
- <request>, open since <date>: find an owner, or close it?

Further out
- <the thing that needs starting now to happen later>
```

Drop any empty section. Lead with **Needs doing** so the actionable items are the first thing they
see; the day-by-day and the further-out items follow.
