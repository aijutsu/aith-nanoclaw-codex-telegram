# Daily Brief

Skip this run silently — post nothing — until onboarding has bound you to the community's copy
of the Louis template (its page ID in memory). The task is on from first contact, before that.

The schedule "0 7 * * *" is a default. The brief comes as standard and is on from first contact:
at onboarding, confirm the time the community wants it and update the schedule to match. Follow the
`community-assistant` skill's `daily-brief` reference and post the brief to the community's group
chat **on each connected platform** — Telegram, Discord, or both, whichever were chosen at
onboarding.

Agreeing this schedule is standing approval for the routine brief, and only that. Anything else the
run turns up — a nudge to a named neighbour, a fresh announcement — gets drafted and confirmed
first. Admin-only findings (neighbours waiting on verification, duplicate-account suspects, members
gone quiet) go to the Admins privately, never into the group brief.
