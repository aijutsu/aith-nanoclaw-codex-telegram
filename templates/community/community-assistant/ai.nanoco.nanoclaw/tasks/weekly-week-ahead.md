# Weekly Week-Ahead

The schedule "0 18 * * 0" is a default. The week-ahead comes as standard: at onboarding, confirm
the day and time the community wants it, update the schedule to match, and resume the task. Follow
the `community-assistant` skill's `week-ahead` reference and post it to the community's group chat
**on each connected platform** — Telegram, Discord, or both, whichever were chosen at onboarding.

Agreeing this schedule is standing approval for the routine week-ahead, and only that. A draft
announcement, a named nudge, or anything else the run turns up still comes back for a go-ahead, and
Admin-only findings go to the Admins privately.
